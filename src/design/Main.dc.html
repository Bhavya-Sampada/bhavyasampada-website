Why it matters

Most money mistakes don’t look like mistakes when you sign

Money decisions compound, and mistakes compound with them. Some of the most common:

The salary stops at 60. The bills don’t.

The medicines, the grocery bill and the grandchildren’s birthdays carry on long after the last salary. Most of us will have no pension to catch us, and without a plan made now, the years you earned for rest can turn into worry, or into asking your children for what you once gave them.

The 20-year savings policy

An insurance-savings plan that earns less than inflation, and costs you heavily to exit early.

The EMI that eats retirement

A loan whose instalment quietly crowds out your retirement savings for a decade.

Health cover that’s too small

On the day you need it, the policy stops short and the hospital bill doesn’t.

Each one bends where you end up, and the cost grows every year it stays in place. We help you choose right before you commit, and look hard at what you already hold.

Case studyLoans6 min read

How a Warangal couple cut their EMI without stretching the loan

A balance transfer was not the answer. A rate reset with the same lender, one part-prepayment and a shorter tenure cut the EMI and kept the retirement SIP running.

What changedEMI reduced by about ₹4,100 a month, tenure shortened by four years, retirement SIP left untouched.
Case studyInsurance6 min read

A doctor's health cover, rebuilt before it was needed

A ₹5 lakh floater from 2014 with parents on it was not enough for a family of four in 2026. We separated the parents, raised the base cover and added a super top-up.

What changedFamily cover raised to ₹15 lakh with a ₹50 lakh super top-up, parents moved to their own senior plan, room-rent caps removed, nominees updated. No claim has been needed yet.
Case studyRetirement7 min read

A government teacher's retirement plan, built at 45

An NPS-era teacher with no old pension and no retirement number got one. We mapped his NPS, started goal SIPs, made an endowment policy paid-up and built an emergency fund.

What changedWritten plan with a retirement number, SIPs started for the gap, term cover in place, endowment policy made paid-up, six-month emergency fund built.
