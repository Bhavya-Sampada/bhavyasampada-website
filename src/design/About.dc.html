Our story

Founded on one belief: every family deserves world-class financial guidance

Bhavya Sampada started in Secunderabad more than fifteen years ago with that belief and a handful of families. Most of them had money in the right places and no plan holding it together, so the first thing we did, and still do, was write the plan before recommending anything.

Over fifteen years the work grew from mutual funds to the whole picture: goal-based planning, every kind of insurance, and loans compared across lenders. What stayed the same is who you deal with. Your first conversation is with a director, and the same people review your plan every six months.

Today we serve more than 2,000 families, in person at our Kapra office and online across India, with ₹250 crore and more entrusted to the plans we manage together.
