# Compliance review of the new site — items to approve before launch

Flagged only; nothing below has been changed in the design except where marked "already applied". References: SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations advertisement code and AMFI's Code of Conduct and guidelines for Mutual Fund Distributors (MFD); SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 on who may call themselves an adviser; IRDAI (Insurance Advertisements and Disclosure) Regulations, 2021; SEBI's 2023 circular on finfluencer and unregistered advice; RBI's digital-lending and fair-practice codes for loan referrals. This is a practitioner's checklist, not legal advice; your compliance person or a SEBI/IRDAI consultant should confirm each decision.

## A. Serious: claims on the CURRENT live site that must not carry over
1. **"SEBI Registered Investment Advisor, Registration No: INA200017566"** (live About page). You told me the firm is an MFD earning commission. An MFD cannot also hold itself out as an RIA, and an RIA cannot earn distribution commission for the same client; SEBI treats a false RIA claim as a serious violation. Either the number is genuine and belongs to a separately registered entity (then it needs its own disclosure and the commission model must be explained), or it must come off. **Not carried over to the new design.** Please confirm which.
2. **"AMFI Certified ... ARN No: 73991"** (live About page). Your ARN is 266666; 73991 is the NJ partner code in your client-login link. Mismatched ARNs on a public page are an AMFI code-of-conduct issue. **Only 266666 is used in the new design.**
3. **"75+ Industry Awards"** with named awards. You've agreed to drop these. **Already removed.**
4. **"Unbiased financial guidance"** and **"without ... conflicts of interest"** (live mission and values). A commission-earning distributor has a conflict by definition; AMFI and SEBI advertising rules bar misleading claims of independence. **Not carried over**; the new values text says "You'll always know how we are paid."
5. **"SEBI registered, AMFI certified"** in the Excellence value. Replaced with no regulator claim in the value; registrations are listed factually in the Registrations block. **Already applied**, approve the wording.

## B. Wording to approve in the new design
6. **"Advisor" / "advice" / "advisory".** SEBI's IA regulations reserve "investment adviser" (and AMFI has told MFDs to avoid "advisor" in names and titles). The new copy uses "directors", "our team", "planning", "review". The legal entity name is "Bhavya Sampada Financial Advisors" in the footer because that is the registered name you gave; if that name is not the registered one, change it. Confirm the legal name.
7. **"Financial planning" as a service.** Goal-based planning incidental to distribution is allowed for an MFD; charging a fee for it is not. The FAQ and Terms say "We do not charge a separate planning fee." Confirm that is true.
8. **Commission disclosure** (Home FAQ "How do you get paid?", Terms §4, footer Regular Plan line). AMFI requires disclosure of commission on mutual funds; the FAQ promises you'll tell clients how each product pays before they commit. Confirm this matches practice.
9. **Insurance wording.** IRDAI's 2021 advertisement regulations require: the intermediary's licence number on advertisements (A00830505 is now in the footer, stat strip, About and Terms); "Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation" (in the footer and Terms); no claim that compares insurers' products as "best". The Services copy says "compared across insurers" and names eight insurers; fine as long as you are licensed to place business with each named insurer. Confirm the licence type (corporate agent, broker, POSP) so the footer can say it exactly; "IRDAI-licensed insurance intermediary" is the placeholder.
10. **IRDAI logo in the stat strip.** IRDAI does not license intermediaries to use its logo, and the regulations bar anything implying regulator endorsement. You've asked to keep it. Flagged; my recommendation is still to show the licence number as text and drop the logo. Your call.
11. **AMFI logo.** AMFI issues a specific "AMFI-registered MFD" mark for ARN holders. The one in the stat strip is AMFI's corporate logo from amfiindia.com. Replace it with the ARN-holder mark from the AMFI distributor portal, or drop it; using AMFI's corporate logo can read as endorsement.
12. **Insurer logos.** Keep only insurers you are actually empanelled with; logos imply a relationship. You've confirmed all eight.
13. **Hero chart ("Two families' investments ... With a Bhavya Sampada plan").** It shows one line rising and one flat, with the firm named. It carries "Illustrative, not a forecast" and no numbers. Under the SEBI advertisement code, illustrations must not imply assured returns; this is borderline because the firm's plan is shown as the cause of the rise. Options: (a) keep, with the note made more prominent and "Past performance is not indicative of future returns" added under the chart; (b) rename the lines "Planned" and "Unplanned" so the firm isn't the variable. Choose one.
14. **Stat claims.** "15+ years", "2,000+ families", "₹250+ Cr AUM", "138 five-star Google reviews". Keep a dated source for each (AUM as of a date from your platform; the review count from Google on the day of launch). AMFI can ask for substantiation.
15. **Case studies.** Must have written client consent (the CMS now blocks publishing without it), no identifiable details beyond what the client agreed, and outcomes limited to actions (cover raised, EMI reduced). No returns or percentages. The design's case-study cards say "Shared with the client's consent. Names changed. Every situation is different." Approve this line.
16. **Google reviews on the site.** Displaying reviews pulled from Google is acceptable; writing them, incentivising them, or filtering out low ratings is not. The design says "Nothing is edited or hand-picked"; the build must honour that (show the latest, not the best). Do not add review/rating schema markup.
17. **Articles disclaimer** (every article): "This article is for information only and is not investment, tax or legal advice. Figures are illustrative. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks ... AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor, ARN 266666." Approve.
18. **Footer disclaimer.** Now reads: AMFI line with ARN · IRDAI licence A00830505 · [Loan registration]. Market-risk line, Regular Plan and trail commission line, "Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation", "Loans are subject to the lender's approval and terms", and the not-advice line. Approve, and supply the loan registration (see 19).
19. **Loans.** If you arrange loans as a lender's agent (DSA) you need each lender's authorisation and RBI's fair-practice disclosures (no promise of approval, rates set by the lender). If you only refer, say so. The Services page and FAQ currently say "we compare lenders"; the footer has a [Loan registration] placeholder. Supply the capacity and reference.
20. **YouTube and Instagram.** The channels are "The Two Wealth Theory". SEBI's rules on finfluencers mean content there must carry the same disclaimers and no return claims; if the channel is a separate brand, a line on the site ("Our channel, The Wealth Theory") avoids confusion. Confirm.
